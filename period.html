🌸 Understanding your cycle

Menstrual cycles can change during the menopausal transition. Tracking your dates can help you notice changes in your own pattern over time.

MenoCare does not use your cycle information to diagnose perimenopause or menopause. Significant, persistent, or concerning changes should be discussed with a healthcare professional.
